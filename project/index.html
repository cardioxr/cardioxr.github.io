Why spatial

Cardiac EP is a relationship between form, field and time.

Flat views are useful, but spatial exploration can make anatomical context and changing field patterns easier to inspect together.

Scientific boundary

Designed to support exploration—not inference.

The current fields are illustrative procedural patterns. They are not patient measurements, a physiological simulation, or a validated clinical solver. CardioXR is not intended for diagnosis or clinical decision-making.

Experimental WebXR

Move around the model. Reach in and manipulate it.

CardioXR reuses the same animated scene in immersive AR, with bounded direct translation, rotation and two-input scaling on compatible WebXR devices.

01

Move

Translate the model within a bounded reachable volume.

Rotate

Use controller squeeze or hand pinch for direct orientation.

03

Scale

Two simultaneous grabs provide restrained uniform scaling.

04

Explore

Keep the active field and temporal playback in immersive AR.

Current status: implemented and emulator-validated. Real-device Android XR/PICO placement, tracking, comfort and platform-specific development remain future work.

Creator

Built at the intersection of engineering, cardiac EP and scientific software.

CardioXR was created by Dr Kęstutis Maciūnas, a biomedical engineer and cardiac electrophysiology researcher at the University of Edinburgh. His work focuses on computational modelling, cardiac arrhythmias and scientific software.

CardioXR is an independent early-stage research and education prototype and is not an official University of Edinburgh product.
