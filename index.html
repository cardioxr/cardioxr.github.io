Early technical demonstrator

Exploring cardiac visualisation in WebXR

CardioXR explores how atrial anatomy and illustrative electrical patterns could be inspected and manipulated in a browser and immersive XR.

Launch interactive demo ↗

What works today

The browser demo supports 3D viewing, illustrative field animation and playback controls. Experimental WebXR adds direct movement, rotation and scaling of the same model. XR interactions have been emulator-tested; real-device validation remains pending.

What hardware testing would establish

The next step is to test placement, tracking, manipulation and comfort on compatible XR hardware, then explore whether spatial inspection is useful for a focused research or teaching activity.

About

Scientific status

The displayed fields are illustrative procedural patterns, not patient measurements or a validated clinical solver. CardioXR is not clinical software and is not intended for diagnosis or clinical decision-making.
