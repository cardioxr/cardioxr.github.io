Early technical demonstrator

Building interactive cardiac tools for education and research

CardioXR is an early technical demonstrator for exploring cardiac anatomy and electrophysiology concepts through interactive 3D models. The current focus is teaching and learning with illustrative models. Future research will explore patient-specific models, quantitative measurements and, longer term, procedure-planning workflows.

Launch interactive demo ↗

Current demonstrator

The current browser-based demonstrator supports interactive 3D viewing of atrial anatomy, illustrative field animation and playback controls. Experimental WebXR extends the same model with direct movement, rotation and scaling. XR interactions have been emulator-tested; validation on physical hardware remains pending.

Next development stage

Near-term work will refine the educational experience and test placement, tracking, manipulation and comfort on physical XR hardware, helping identify where spatial interaction adds value alongside desktop and mobile interfaces. Patient-specific models and quantitative measurements remain later research aims; procedure-planning workflows are a longer-term research direction.

Scientific status
